## Sceptical self-review and limitations

This is **self-review, not independent review**. A aligns better with durable journal use; B is visually immediate for identification intent but could attract more one-off use. No empirical uplift is established. At 390px with first-visit consent, the badge-link bottom moves from about 733px in the baseline to 551px (A) / 509px (B). A is slightly lower than the saved-consent baseline, so no blanket claim that every state is improved is made. Final keyboard review moved the badge focus outline inside its clear-space container to prevent the left edge being cut off on narrow A; actual Tab navigation to both primary badges was then recaptured and passed. This focus-only correction does not alter normal layout.

Mobile prioritises value/download over showing a complete product screen above the fold. At large text the primary badge and consent move further down while the header download stays early. At 320px normal size the consent choices can require scrolling. No fixed overlay obstructs content. The desktop proof remains clearer than mobile screenshot text, and raster evidence does not respond to browser font scaling; original-image links help inspection but are not a substitute for real accessible app UI.

The approved existing artwork is truthful but older than the unreconciled current release report. Fresh approved populated current-version imagery remains a potential later improvement. No private catches or locations were accessed to fill that gap. Physical Android, Safari, Firefox, screen-reader runtime, field performance and measured conversion are unverified.
