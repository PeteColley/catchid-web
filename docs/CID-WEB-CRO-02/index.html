Recommended direction: Concept A

A presents the durable reason to download CatchID: keeping a useful personal fishing journal. Its headline also explains optional identification in the first glance. The populated journal makes that promise tangible without making recognition accuracy the main selling point.

This is a design judgement, not measured conversion uplift. B may appeal more strongly to visitors arriving with an immediate fish-identification question. Audience intent and real qualified Play clicks would need measurement after a separately approved implementation.
